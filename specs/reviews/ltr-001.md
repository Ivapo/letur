**Where the build departed from the plan, each for a reason found while building:**

- **The loops still guard on the absolute path.** The plan made `edited` root-relative
  and would have compared that; two projects each holding a `README.md` would then
  pass a leftover loop's guard. `Session` joins it onto the root instead, which is the
  comparison the loops made before, and the `Gate` cases are unchanged.
- **The delete is a closure `Preview::trash` is handed**, not `Files::remove` alone:
  the desktop's suite replaces the move to the Trash per call, and `Session::trash`'s
  signature, `main.rs` and the three clauses that hand in a double stay as they were.
- **`Files::locate` and a `read` of a located path** replaced a purely root-relative
  read, so `render_with`, `read_sections_with` and `read_assets_with` keep their closure
  seam and the sentences keep the desktop's absolute path by construction.
- **`default_output` stays a function** (`project/src/document.rs`), which
  `Preview::export_path` asks — the specs cite it by name.
- **Two additions to the crate's surface**: the four sentences are `pub const`, for the
  desktop's tests to assert against; and `Preview::hold`, `#[doc(hidden)]`, moves the
  pane without loading, for the one `Gate` case that must move it without a compile.
- **`clippy.toml` also lists the `Path` methods that stat** (`is_file`, `exists`,
  `canonicalize` …): the same calls as `std::fs`'s, reached from a path.
- **`http` is the crate's third dependency** — `remote::host` parses a URL with it,
  where `app` reached it as `ureq::http`. It was already in `Cargo.lock`.
